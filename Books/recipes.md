# My Weekly Recipes

*7 meals for 1 — pasta, pasta salad, and a Caesar.*

---

## 1. Cacio e Pepe

*Three ingredients, one of Rome's greatest pasta dishes. All about technique.*

**Serves:** 1 | **Time:** ~20 mins

### Ingredients
- 100g tagliatelle
- 30g pecorino, finely grated
- 20g parmesan, finely grated
- 1½ tsp black pepper, coarsely ground
- 100ml pasta water (reserved)
- 1 tsp salt

### Method
1. **Toast the pepper** — Add pepper to a dry pan on medium heat. Toast for 60 seconds until fragrant. Set aside half, leave half in the pan.
2. **Boil the pasta** — Bring a pot of water to the boil, salt lightly (less than usual — the cheese is salty). Cook tagliatelle until just under al dente. Reserve at least 100ml of pasta water before draining.
3. **Make the cheese paste** — Mix pecorino and parmesan in a bowl. Add 3–4 tbsp of hot pasta water and stir into a smooth, creamy paste. It should be thick but pourable.
4. **Combine** — Add drained pasta to the pan with toasted pepper. Add a splash of pasta water and toss. Remove the pan from the heat entirely — this is critical. Add the cheese paste and toss vigorously, adding more pasta water a splash at a time until you have a silky, glossy sauce.
5. **Serve** — Plate immediately, top with the reserved cracked pepper and extra pecorino.

> **Tip:** The pan must be off the heat when you add the cheese, or it will scramble. Finely grating the cheese (small side of the grater) is what makes the paste smooth.

## 2. Carbonara

*The real Roman carbonara — no cream, ever. Rich, silky, and ready in 20 minutes.*

**Serves:** 1 | **Time:** ~20 mins

### Ingredients
- 100g tagliatelle
- 80g guanciale or pancetta, diced
- 2 egg yolks
- 25g pecorino, finely grated
- 15g parmesan, finely grated
- 1 tsp black pepper, coarsely ground
- 80ml pasta water (reserved)

### Method
1. **Render the guanciale** — Put guanciale in a cold pan and bring to medium heat. Let it render slowly in its own fat for 8–10 minutes until golden and crispy at the edges. Remove from heat — you want it warm, not screaming hot.
2. **Make the egg mixture** — Whisk egg yolks with pecorino, parmesan, and pepper in a bowl until combined. It will be thick — that's fine.
3. **Cook the pasta** — Boil tagliatelle in well-salted water until al dente. Reserve 80ml pasta water before draining.
4. **Combine off the heat** — Add drained pasta to the pan with guanciale. Toss. Let the pan cool for 30 seconds — crucial. Add the egg mixture and toss quickly, adding pasta water a splash at a time until the sauce is smooth, glossy, and clings to the pasta. If it looks scrambled, the pan was too hot.
5. **Serve** — Plate immediately, top with any remaining guanciale, extra pecorino, and a generous crack of black pepper.

> **Tip:** Two yolks for one portion gives a richer sauce than using a whole egg. Guanciale (cured pork cheek) is traditional and worth finding — it has more flavour than pancetta.

## 3. Aglio e Olio

*Garlic, olive oil, chilli, parsley. Barely 15 minutes, barely any effort.*

**Serves:** 1 | **Time:** ~15 mins

### Ingredients
- 100g spaghetti
- 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 3 tbsp good olive oil
- ½ tsp chilli flakes
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, roughly chopped
- 60ml pasta water (reserved)
- 1 tsp salt

### Method
1. **Cook the pasta** — Boil spaghetti in generously salted water until al dente. Reserve 60ml pasta water before draining.
2. **Infuse the oil** — While the pasta cooks, warm olive oil in a pan on medium-low. Add garlic and chilli flakes. Cook gently for 3–4 minutes, stirring, until the garlic is pale golden. Do not let it brown.
3. **Bring it together** — Add drained pasta to the pan with a good splash of pasta water. Toss over medium heat for 1 minute until the oil and water emulsify into a light sauce.
4. **Serve** — Remove from heat, add parsley and toss once more. Finish with a tiny drizzle of raw olive oil.

> **Tip:** The difference between golden and burnt garlic is about 20 seconds — watch it carefully. Use your best olive oil here, it is the sauce.

## 4. Tuna Pasta

*A proper fridge-raid pasta. Salty, savoury, and done in 20 minutes.*

**Serves:** 1 | **Time:** ~20 mins

### Ingredients
- 100g penne
- 145g tinned tuna in olive oil
- 200g tinned chopped tomatoes
- 2 garlic cloves, sliced
- 1 tbsp capers
- 8 black olives, halved
- 1½ tbsp olive oil
- ¼ tsp chilli flakes (optional)
- 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

### Method
1. **Make the sauce** — Warm olive oil in a pan on medium. Add garlic and chilli flakes and fry for 1 minute. Add chopped tomatoes, capers, and olives. Simmer for 10 minutes until slightly thickened.
2. **Cook the pasta** — While the sauce simmers, cook penne in well-salted boiling water until al dente. Drain.
3. **Finish** — Remove the sauce from the heat. Flake in the tuna (including the oil from the tin) and stir gently. Add the drained pasta and toss to coat.
4. **Serve** — Plate and scatter with parsley. No cheese — tuna and parmesan don't mix.

> **Tip:** Don't drain the tuna — the olive oil from the tin goes into the sauce. Add the tuna off the heat to keep it in bigger flakes rather than mush.

## 5. Arrabiata

*Fiery, simple, and better than any jarred arrabiata sauce.*

**Serves:** 1 | **Time:** ~20 mins

### Ingredients
- 100g penne
- 200g tinned chopped tomatoes
- 3 garlic cloves, sliced
- ¾ tsp chilli flakes
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tbsp fresh parsley or basil, to serve

### Method
1. **Fry the garlic and chilli** — Warm olive oil in a pan on medium. Add garlic and chilli flakes. Fry gently for 2 minutes, stirring, until the garlic softens but doesn't colour.
2. **Simmer the sauce** — Add chopped tomatoes and salt. Stir well. Simmer on medium-low for 12–15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens and darkens slightly.
3. **Cook the pasta** — While the sauce simmers, cook penne in well-salted boiling water until al dente. Drain, reserving a splash of pasta water.
4. **Combine and serve** — Add the pasta to the sauce and toss over medium heat for 1 minute, adding a splash of pasta water if needed. Plate and top with parsley or basil.

> **Tip:** Don't rush the garlic and chilli in the oil — that's where all the flavour comes from. Adjust chilli to your heat preference.

## 6. Pesto Pasta Salad

*Great as a meal on its own, or make ahead for lunch the next day.*

**Serves:** 1 | **Time:** ~15 mins

### Ingredients
- 100g fusilli
- 2 tbsp pesto
- 10 cherry tomatoes, halved
- 60g mozzarella pearls
- 30g baby spinach
- 6 black olives, halved
- ½ tsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. **Cook the pasta** — Cook fusilli in well-salted boiling water until al dente. Drain and rinse briefly under cold water. Drizzle with olive oil and toss to stop it sticking.
2. **Dress the pasta** — In a bowl, combine cooled pasta with pesto and lemon juice. Toss well to coat every piece.
3. **Add the rest** — Add cherry tomatoes, mozzarella pearls, spinach, and olives. Fold gently so you don't break up the mozzarella.
4. **Season and serve** — Taste and adjust seasoning. Serve straight away or refrigerate for up to a day.

> **Tip:** If making ahead, hold back the spinach and mozzarella and add them just before eating so they don't wilt or go rubbery.

## 7. Caesar-ish Salad

*A proper Caesar dressing — no bottled stuff, made in minutes in a bowl.*

**Serves:** 1 | **Time:** ~15 mins

### Ingredients
- ½ head romaine lettuce, roughly torn
- 20g parmesan, shaved or grated
- 2 slices baguette or sourdough, torn into chunks
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 egg yolk
- 2 anchovy fillets
- ½ garlic clove
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- ½ tsp Dijon mustard
- ½ tsp Worcestershire sauce
- Black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. **Make the croutons** — Toss bread chunks with half the olive oil and a pinch of salt. Bake at 200°C for 8–10 minutes until golden and crisp, or fry in a pan on medium-high for 4–5 minutes, tossing regularly.
2. **Make the dressing** — Mash anchovies and garlic into a paste with the back of a fork. Add egg yolk, lemon juice, mustard, and Worcestershire sauce. Whisk together, then slowly drizzle in the remaining olive oil while whisking until you have a thick, emulsified dressing. Season with black pepper.
3. **Assemble** — Add lettuce to a large bowl. Pour over the dressing and toss well — every leaf should be coated. Add most of the parmesan and toss again.
4. **Serve** — Pile into a bowl or plate. Top with croutons and remaining parmesan. Eat immediately.

> **Tip:** If you don't want raw egg yolk in the dressing, swap it for a tablespoon of mayonnaise. The anchovy is not optional — it's what makes it taste like Caesar.
